# Board Rules (v1) — /r/board

*Taproot deployment policy. Framework default; node operators may override.*

## Rules for anonymous posting

1. Nothing illegal under US law.
2. No NSFW.
3. No spamming; no flooding that compromises normal operation of the site.

## Enforcement

- Anonymous posts are allowed on board-archetype roots only.
- Board posts roll off after retention window (Taproot: 24 hours).
- Moderators may delete posts in their root at any time.
- Admin token may delete any post.
